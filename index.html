What to check before ordering

Save the listing and variant

Open a CAFANS product page and locate its original marketplace listing. Save the URL, color and size together. For tops, compare chest width, length and shoulders with a garment you own. For shoes, compare your foot length with the seller’s size chart.

Plan your warehouse QC photos

Check what photos your agent offers. For shoes, request size-label and outsole photos. For clothing, check the seams, color and measurements. If you spot a mismatch, check the return or exchange deadline before approving dispatch.

Compare shipping quotes

Ask for the packed weight and dimensions once your items are ready. Check how each shipping line calculates billable weight and whether the quote includes insurance or taxes. Compare quotes for the same destination and parcel.

Order budget calculator

Enter estimates in USD. Include taxes or duties in other charges if they are separate from the shipping quote.

KakoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Where do the product links go?

The product links open CAFANS detail pages. Check the original marketplace link and the available options there before placing an order through your chosen agent.

Does the listed price include shipping?

The directory shows reference item prices. Check the seller’s domestic delivery charge and get a separate quote for international shipping and any agent fees.

What should I do if an item or variant is unavailable?

Open the matching CAFANS category to look for another listing. Compare the new seller’s size chart and confirm availability before ordering.
